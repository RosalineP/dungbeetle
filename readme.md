# DUNGAMARI DUNGACY

# General brain weather:
Rather than immediately absorbing an enemy, when you it them they turn into
a pile of pellets. At small scales, they're generally where the enemy was anyway
so you can absorb them easily, but for larger enemies there's more spread.
This could also be a cool way to animate the enemy death and size increase--ball
converts to particles of dung, the little beetle runs away.

# Progression ideas:
Two slots for activated abilities:
1. Lunge forward,
2. push enemies away,
3. Slime trail that slows enemies,
4. Shoot projectile that converts half of an enemy's mass into scattered pellets

Getting another instance of the same ability will upgrade it. Once you have two abilities at level 3,
they combine, freeing up one of the slots for a new ability.

The combinations are as follows:

1+2 = Lunge forward that pushes only larger enemies away, leaving smaller enemies unaffected.
1+3 = Lunge forward that leaves a slime trail behind, slowing enemies that walk over it.
1+4 = Lunge forward and any larger enemies in your path have a portion of mass converted to pellets.

2+3 = Spray slime outwards, pushing enemies away and leaving a big slime patch.
2+4 = Push enemies away and convert a portion of their mass into pellets.

3+4 = Enemies the walk on the slime trail slowly shed mass as pellets.

Passive abilities:
1. Speed increase
2. Agility increase (turning and acceleration)
3. Armor--instead of dying, you lose a portion of your mass.
4. Pellet collection distance.
